A stand-in page for previewing the widget

The button at the bottom right is the one that appears on a book page. The background switches at the top left: the glass has to read on all three. The pane can be dragged — it lands in the nearest corner; Alt+arrows do the same from the keyboard.

Filler text, so the page looks like a chapter and the widget has something to lie on.

The rain had started in the night and showed no sign of stopping, and by morning the porch had turned slippery. She stood at the railing and watched the water collect in the rut under the old apple tree, and thought she should have left yesterday.

The door creaked behind her. He came out without a coat and stood beside her — close enough that she could hear him breathe, and far enough that she did not have to turn around.

— You have decided, then, he said. Not a question.

The wind threw a handful of cold drops into her face. Somewhere behind the house, in the wet dark, a chain clanked, and the dog, having rattled it, went quiet again.
